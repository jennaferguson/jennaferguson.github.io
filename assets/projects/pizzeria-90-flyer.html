The Story

Behind the project

When Bistro 90 opened its sister restaurant Pizzeria 90, it needed a flyer that would build buzz before opening day. The design connects the new shop to Bistro 90's existing reputation ("Bistro 90 brings you…") while giving Pizzeria 90 its own energetic look.

Instant headlineChunky yellow "PIZZERIA 90" type with a deep red shadow reads from across a room.
What's on offerPizza, Subs, Wings, Salads & More are stacked in outline type so the menu is clear in seconds.
UrgencyA burgundy "Coming This October" badge builds anticipation.
Ready to actThe address and the memorable 843-399-PIZZA number anchor the bottom.
