Project Overview

Research meets brand personality.

Created a social media strategy and brand positioning plan for a fictional dog apparel brand as part of a social media marketing course. The project included researching competitors across Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook, identifying opportunities to differentiate the brand, developing target audience personas, and establishing a brand voice and content strategy.

Based on the competitive analysis, I proposed ways to build brand awareness and audience engagement through relatable content, humor, user-generated content, and community interaction. I also developed SMART campaign objectives and key performance indicators to measure potential campaign success.

Academic group project for a fictional brand. Objectives below are proposed targets, not achieved campaign results.

The Strategy

From audit to action.

Competitive analysisReviewed Canada Pooch, Lucy & Co., and Wild One across Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook to compare content, posting frequency, engagement, and opportunities for differentiation.
Brand positioningPositioned the dog hoodie around the bond between dogs and their owners, moving beyond product-only promotion toward personality and community.
Audience researchDeveloped consumer personas covering lifestyle, motivations, shopping behavior, social media habits, and practical apparel needs.
Brand voicePlayful, relatable, authentic, warm, and community-driven, with humor and everyday dog-owner experiences at the center.
Content planningProposed short-form videos, relatable dog-owner moments, customer-submitted photos, polls, and community features.
Campaign developmentConnected SMART objectives to reach, impressions, comments, shares, saves, follower growth, and user-generated content.
Proposed Campaign

Clear goals. Measurable progress.

30 daysProposed campaign window, with a day-one baseline for comparison.
15% growthTarget increase in reach and impressions.
10% growthTarget increase in engagement such as comments, shares, and saves.
3–5 posts per weekProposed publishing cadence focused on humor, relatability, and community participation.
The goal: make dog apparel part of a community people want to join, not just a product they see in their feed.
