The Story

Behind the project

Lucky's Garage is a local mobile mechanic, so the logo needed to look great on a work truck, a business card, and a Facebook profile picture. It also needed to stand out from typical auto-shop branding.

The name, visualizedFuzzy red dice hang off the script lettering, a playful nod to "Lucky" and classic car culture.
Racing heritageCheckered flags signal speed, motorsport, and automotive know-how at a glance.
Retro script + bold blockVintage red script for personality, paired with heavy italic "GARAGE" type for strength and readability.
Limited paletteRed, black, and cream keep it high contrast and easy to reproduce on decals, shirts, and signage.
