Owala Social Media Strategy

Social Media Strategy

A one-page social strategy to grow Owala's online sales among college students through educational and UGC TikToks.

StatusAcademic Project
RoleStrategist
ToolsCanva
TypeStrategy one-sheet

Independent academic assignment. Brand imagery is shown for analysis; this is not commissioned work or a brand endorsement.

The Story

Behind the project

Business goal: increase online purchases of Owala water bottles among college-aged consumers.
Target audienceCollege students ages 18–24 who are into fitness and wellness, fashion, and lifestyle, and who use social media often.
Platform: TikTokPopular with college-aged consumers, its visual format works well, and it supports product discovery and online purchases.
Content strategyEducational TikToks showing how Owala features make hydration easier on a busy school day, plus UGC of students using and personalizing their bottles.
MeasurementEngagement rate for interaction, and conversion rate for purchases driven by posts, tying directly back to the sales goal.
