The Story

Behind the project

Pizzeria 90 (7066 Hwy 90, Longs SC) uses this two-page menu with real customers every day. The challenge was fitting a full pizzeria menu (pizzas in three sizes, toppings, salads, subs, wings, dinners, sides, and desserts) into a layout that's easy to scan at the counter.

Clear sectionsSalads, Pizza, Toppings, Hot & Cold Subs, Wings, Dinner, Sides, and Desserts each get their own labeled block.
Easy price scanningThe pizza section uses 12" / 16" / 20" price columns so guests can compare sizes instantly.
Signature pies up frontHouse specialties like The 90, The Sinatra, The Roxy, and The Meatlover get descriptions that sell.
Contact on every pagePhone (843-399-PIZZA) and address sit in the header bar of both pages for take-out orders.
