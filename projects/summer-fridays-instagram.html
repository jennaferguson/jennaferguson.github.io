The Story

Behind the project

Post A: effectiveThe jeweled Jet Lag eye-patch cover image taps a trending "whimsical gems" aesthetic and feels Pinterest-worthy.
Timing & trendsCoastal European summer imagery and the line "made for wherever summer takes you" ride the viral travel trend.
Not too pushyThe carousel shows the product while staying on theme, so it feels like content instead of a hard sell.
Post B: less effectiveA clean studio product shot looks polished but lacks the human element, so it reads as an ad people scroll past.
Takeaway: on Instagram, posts have to be on trend, visually appealing, and feel human to earn the click, especially with new audiences.
